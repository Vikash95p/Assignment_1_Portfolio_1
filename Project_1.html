My Development Approach

I approach projects through a combination of self-learning, experimentation, practical development, and AI-assisted learning.

  1. Understand the problem and define the project goal.
  2. Research the required concepts and technologies.
  3. Design and implement the core functionality.
  4. Test the project and troubleshoot errors.
  5. Improve the implementation through experimentation.

AI tools are used as learning and development assistants. The focus is on understanding concepts and exploring their practical implementation.
